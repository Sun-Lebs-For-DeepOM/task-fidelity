# Privacy and release scope

This public release contains the skill instructions, supporting workflow references, presentation metadata, installation documentation, and illustrative examples.

It excludes original conversations, local task histories, personal research materials, promotion drafts, downloaded memes, account credentials, browser state, and machine-specific configuration.

Examples are constructed to explain decisions and output checks. They are not transcripts of model evaluation runs.

When sharing an issue or example, include only the minimum task inputs needed to reproduce the problem and remove confidential material first. The skill itself does not collect or transmit telemetry.

The source and design references in `references/method-basis.md` are links and brief adaptations. Full third-party papers or images are not distributed in this repository.
